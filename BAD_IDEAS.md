# Ideas we decided against

Kept for the record only. Not part of the plan; don't build from these unless asked.

## Game concepts

- Porting erase or shadow-siege: neither fits a daily, under-two-minute format.
- Fault Line: a shared map where each player gets one move a day.
- Forgery: draw something from memory.
- Blind Auction.
- Upvote: rank fake post titles by a hidden rule.
- Thread: rebuild a comment reply chain by deduction, possibly with fictional or user-submitted threads.
- Karma Grid: Kakuro with reaction tiles.
- Yesterday's top comment becomes today's puzzle.
- Players voting on the funniest wrong guesses: free-text guesses would need moderation. Replaced by the wrong-guess chart.
- Snoo with bunny ears as the logo: Snoo is Reddit's trademark, so the logo stays our own line-drawn bunny.

## Names not chosen

Runners-up: Lurker, Inside Joke, Where Am I, Tourist.

Also considered: Which Sub, Sidebar, Karma Compass, Drift, Threadfinder, Snoop, Lost and Founded, Pathfinder, Crowdsight, Neighbourhood Watch, Hivemind, Mystery Sub, Down the Subhole.
