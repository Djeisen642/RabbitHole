Meet the bunny

One unbroken line, redrawn every frame. Tap a move to see it the way it would play during a round.

Key poses

  • The whole bunny is one SVG path. Ears and foot move by bending that path, so it never splits into pieces.
  • No animation library. About 15 KB of plain JavaScript (5 KB gzipped) drives every move.
  • With reduced motion on, poses switch without movement and the dive becomes a fade.
  • If you leave it alone for 15 seconds, it starts thumping.
